The Painted Bible

Bible stories through the great paintings. KJV verses, public domain art.

The Painted Bible tells one Bible story at a time in short videos: a King James Version passage, narrated over a public-domain masterpiece by painters such as Gustave Doré, Rembrandt and Thomas Cole. Every artwork is credited, and every verse is quoted from the KJV.
