Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy covers the website of The Painted Bible and Painted Bible Publisher, the internal tool we use to publish our own videos to our own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram.

Who uses the tool

Painted Bible Publisher is used only by The Painted Bible's owner to manage The Painted Bible's own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it does not sign in or collect data from any other person.

What data the tool accesses

  • Access tokens issued by YouTube (Google), TikTok and Meta when the owner authorizes the tool for The Painted Bible's own accounts.
  • Basic information about those accounts (account id, name) and statistics about our own posts (views, likes, watch time), used to decide what to publish next.

How it is used and stored

Tokens and statistics are stored on a private server controlled by the owner, with file permissions restricted to the owner. They are used only to upload our videos, set their titles and descriptions, and read their performance. We do not sell, share or transfer this data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google user data

Painted Bible Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool uses the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Deletion and revocation

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from Google account permissions, TikTok's app settings or Meta's business integrations settings. Revoked tokens are deleted from our server.

Website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers. It loads its typefaces from Google Fonts and is hosted on GitHub Pages; both may keep standard server logs (see Google Fonts privacy and GitHub’s privacy statement).

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message to The Painted Bible on Facebook.
